    Read this before you rely on it. State aviation tax rules change and turn on facts specific to your deal — the seller's status, where the plane sleeps, whether you ever put it on leaseback. The numbers here are researched estimates from state revenue departments, state DOT aviation offices, and AOPA's state advocacy pages as of August 2026, not tax advice. Before you close, confirm your basing state's rules with an aviation tax attorney or CPA. A one-hour consult costs less than any of the mistakes on this page.
